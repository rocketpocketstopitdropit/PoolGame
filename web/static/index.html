On iPhone: tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen to install Pool like an app (full screen, works offline).

Turn your phone sideways to play. Drag on the table to aim, fine-tune with the wheel, touch the cue ball on the right to add spin, raise the cue angle for a jump shot, then pull the power bar down and let go to shoot. With ball in hand, drag the cue ball to place it. You can hold the power bar and aim with another finger.
