POOL

8-Ball · 9-Ball · trick shots · practice

GAME

Trick shots: half the table's missing between the break area and the far end. Jump the cue ball across the gap and sink the 1. Five levels, taking turns with the computer or a friend: whoever makes it in fewer shots wins the level, and most levels wins the match.

OPPONENT
COMPUTER SKILL
CALL POCKET

Tap a pocket to call it. A ball in the wrong pocket is slop: your turn ends (the 8 in the wrong pocket loses; a 9 in the wrong pocket is respotted).

CONTROLS

On iPhone: tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen to install Pool like an app (full screen, works offline).

Turn your phone sideways to play. Rock, paper, scissors decides who breaks. Drag on the table to aim, fine-tune with the wheel (slow drags are extra fine, fast swipes swing the cue a long way), touch the cue ball on the right to add spin, slide the JUMP slider up for a jump shot, then pull the power bar down and let go to shoot. With ball in hand, drag the cue ball to place it. You can hold the power bar and aim with another finger.
